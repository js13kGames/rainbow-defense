Defend the rainbow crystal from corrupted unicorn hordes. Cleanse enemies into points, build Prism and Slow towers, and upgrade your physical rainbow blaster.

Unlock the shotgun at wave 3 and the sniper at wave 5. Survive all six waves to face the Alicorn.

**How to play**
- VR: grab the gun, eject the empty magazine, take a fresh magazine from your hip, insert it, and release. The gun chambers automatically.
- Let your towers cover reloads. Towers earn points by cleansing unicorns.

Built with A-Frame for WebXR, with procedural game geometry, effects, and audio.
